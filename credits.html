City locations are adapted from GeoNames, using the cities500 dataset downloaded September 12, 2026, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.

AcreTown filtered the dataset to United States places, selected city name, state and coordinates, and ordered records by population for city search. Some smaller places are not included.

The GeoNames data is provided as is, without warranty or a representation of accuracy, timeliness or completeness. City markers show approximate city locations. Distances to properties are measured in a straight line.

Property information is provided by AcreTown. Google Maps supplies the map imagery and displays its own required attribution on the map.
